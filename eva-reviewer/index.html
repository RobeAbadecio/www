Formula reference & glossary
(CV) Cost Variance(EV) Earned Value − (AC) Actual Cost
(SV) Schedule Variance(EV) Earned Value − (PV) Planned Value
(CPI) Cost Performance Index(EV) Earned Value ÷ (AC) Actual Cost
(SPI) Schedule Performance Index(EV) Earned Value ÷ (PV) Planned Value
(EAC) Estimate at Completion — current cost efficiency continues(BAC) Budget at Completion ÷ (CPI) Cost Performance Index
(EAC) Estimate at Completion — remaining work at planned cost(AC) Actual Cost + [(BAC) Budget at Completion − (EV) Earned Value]
(ETC) Estimate to Complete(EAC) Estimate at Completion − (AC) Actual Cost
(VAC) Variance at Completion(BAC) Budget at Completion − (EAC) Estimate at Completion
(TCPI) To-Complete Performance Index — original budget target[(BAC) Budget at Completion − (EV) Earned Value] ÷ [(BAC) Budget at Completion − (AC) Actual Cost]
(TCPI) To-Complete Performance Index — revised target[(BAC) Budget at Completion − (EV) Earned Value] ÷ [revised (EAC) Estimate at Completion − (AC) Actual Cost]
(EAC) Estimate at Completion — combined performance model(AC) Actual Cost + [(BAC) Budget at Completion − (EV) Earned Value] ÷ [(CPI) Cost Performance Index × (SPI) Schedule Performance Index]
